## Online advertising in Guyana

**Online advertising is paying to put your business in front of the right people on platforms like Facebook, Instagram and Google, targeted by location, interests and behaviour.** For businesses in Guyana, Facebook and Instagram are usually the best place to start, because that's where most people spend their time online and where many buying decisions begin.

Unlike a billboard or a radio spot, online ads can be aimed at exactly who you want to reach, such as homeowners in Georgetown, travellers planning a trip to Guyana or businesses on the East Coast, and every result can be measured.

## Social media that sends people somewhere

**Social media marketing is using platforms like Facebook and Instagram to reach new customers and keep existing ones coming back.** Paid ads reach new people quickly, while regular posts keep your business in front of the people who already follow you.

But likes and followers don't pay the bills. The point of social media is to get the right people to take the next step: visit your website, send a WhatsApp message or call.

## Why ads and websites work better together

Most social media agencies don't build websites, and most web designers don't run ads. That gap is where budgets disappear: ads send people to a slow page, or to a Facebook page with no clear next step, and nobody can tell which ads actually worked.

We do both. Your campaigns point to pages built to convert, with tracking in place, so every dollar can be traced to a result.
