## Social media that sends people somewhere

**Social media marketing is using platforms like Facebook and Instagram to reach new customers and keep existing ones coming back.** In Guyana, these are where most people spend their time online, and where many buying decisions start.

But likes and followers don't pay the bills. The point of social media is to get the right people to take the next step: visit your website, send a WhatsApp message or call.

## Why ads and websites work better together

Most social media agencies don't build websites, and most web designers don't run ads. That gap is where budgets disappear: ads send people to a slow page, or to a Facebook page with no clear next step, and nobody can tell which ads actually worked.

We do both. Your campaigns point to pages built to convert, with tracking in place, so every dollar can be traced to a result.

## How we work

1. **Understand the goal.** More bookings, more quote requests, more walk-ins? We start there.
2. **Set up tracking.** The Meta Pixel and conversion events on your website, so results can be measured.
3. **Create and launch.** Content and ad creative that fit your brand, aimed at the right audience.
4. **Measure and improve.** We review results, cut what isn't working and scale what is.
