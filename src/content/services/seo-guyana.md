## What is SEO?

**SEO (search engine optimization) is the work of making your website easy for Google to find, understand and trust, so it shows your business when people search for what you sell.** For most businesses in Guyana, that means appearing when someone types "AC repair Georgetown", "tour operator Guyana" or "generator supplier near me".

A beautiful website that doesn't appear in those searches only gets visitors you send it yourself. SEO is how it starts bringing in customers who have never heard of you.

## Why local SEO matters in Guyana

Search in Guyana is local and competitive in a small number of places: the Google Maps pack, the first few organic results, and now AI answers in Google and ChatGPT. Businesses that show up there get the calls. Businesses that don't, rely on referrals and Facebook.

The good news is that most Guyanese websites still get the basics wrong: missing page titles, no Google Business Profile, duplicate versions of the site, or no information about where the business is. Fixing those basics is often enough to move ahead of competitors.
