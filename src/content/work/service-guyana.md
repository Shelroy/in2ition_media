## The goal

Service Guyana is a Georgetown-based HVAC and mechanical contractor. It designs, supplies, installs and maintains air conditioning, building controls and indoor air quality systems for commercial, residential, industrial, marine and offshore clients across Guyana.

The website had to do two jobs. For large companies, it needed to show that Service Guyana is an established, authorized HVAC contractor that serious businesses already trust. For anyone with a broken system, it needed to make the 24-hour emergency line impossible to miss.

## What we built

A clean, corporate HVAC website in Service Guyana's navy and green brand colours. The homepage opens with an image-led hero showing their 24-hour service and HVAC equipment, followed by a short "Who We Are" introduction that says exactly what the company does and where.

Below that, the four core services (design, sales, installation and maintenance) are laid out as clear cards. A wall of client logos shows the oil and gas, offshore, retail and hospitality companies Service Guyana works with. The page closes with a strong "Call or Schedule an Appointment" section, so every visit ends with a next step.

Separate pages cover the company's background, its expertise, projects and a photo gallery, giving Google more relevant content about HVAC and mechanical contracting in Guyana.

## Why it works

For an HVAC contractor in Guyana, trust and speed decide who gets the call. Service Guyana's website answers both straight away: recognisable clients and dealer brands build confidence, and the emergency line is visible on every screen.
