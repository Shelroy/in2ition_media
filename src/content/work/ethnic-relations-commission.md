## The goal

The Ethnic Relations Commission (ERC) is a constitutional body, established under Article 212 D of the Constitution of Guyana, with a mandate to promote ethnic harmony and good relations among all Guyanese.

Its website serves a wide public: people who believe they have faced discrimination because of their race, religion or culture, people reporting discrimination against others, and organisations working for harmonious ethnic relations. It had to explain the Commission's role plainly, show its work across the regions, and make filing a complaint simple.

## What we built

A clear, easy-to-use government website in the ERC's teal and gold colours. The homepage opens with a photo slider of the Commission's community work, followed by a "What's Happening Right Now" news section with the latest press releases.

At the heart of the site is the online complaint system. Instead of a single long form, it guides people through seven short steps, from their own details to the outcome they're hoping for, with evidence uploads. A complaint can be started from the homepage or from the About Us menu.

Around it, the site covers who the Commission is, its Commissioners and staff, careers, events, galleries and videos, published reports and newsletters, an FAQ and contact details, with a newsletter sign-up so the public can follow its work.

## Why it works

For a public body, the website is often the first point of contact. The ERC's site makes the Commission's mandate easy to understand, keeps its work visible, and turns reporting discrimination into something anyone can do from a phone.
