## The goal

GCMC is a general contracting and construction management firm in Georgetown with over a decade of experience, covering commercial construction, renovations, property development, AC service and pool service.

Their previous website dated from 2022. It had the right information, but dozens of strong site photos were sitting in a plain gallery, and nothing on the page showed visitors what GCMC actually builds or how they run a job.

## What we built

A modern, photo-led website with a dark, confident look that takes its blue from the GCMC logo. The homepage opens with a full-screen slideshow of finished interiors, then moves through the company's approach, six service cards, a scrolling strip of recent projects and a before-and-after slider of the same lobby mid-build and finished.

Every service has its own section with the full details, and the projects area is organised into case studies. The cinema fit-out follows the build stage by stage, and a residential case study covers kitchens, bathrooms and living spaces. A filterable gallery with a full-screen viewer holds every photo.

Animation is used with restraint: headlines reveal word by word, photos wipe in as you scroll, and pages fade into each other. Anyone who prefers reduced motion gets a still version of the site.

## Why it works

People hiring a contractor want proof. GCMC's new site leads with real projects, shows the work at every stage, and keeps a phone number and quote form one tap away, so a visitor who likes what they see can get in touch straight away.
