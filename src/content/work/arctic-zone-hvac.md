## The goal

Arctic Zone works on hospitals, hotels, factories, warehouses and office buildings, where downtime is expensive and trust matters. The website needed to make that expertise obvious in seconds and give every kind of visitor a clear next step: request a quote, call the emergency line, or get an estimate.

## What we built

We designed the site around the decisions a facility manager makes. The homepage opens with a strong statement of what Arctic Zone does and who it's for, then moves through the industries it serves, its core services, its process and recent projects.

The **cooling estimator** is the centrepiece. Instead of a generic contact form, visitors can size their cooling need themselves. That's useful on its own, and it leads naturally into a quote request.

Everything is hand-coded, responsive and structured for search, so the site loads fast on mobile data and gives Google clear signals about Arctic Zone's services in Guyana.
