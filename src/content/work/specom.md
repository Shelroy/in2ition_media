## The goal

Specom supplies, installs and maintains critical power systems for businesses that can't afford downtime. They represent a long list of international brands and offer a wide range of services, and the website needed to present all of that without overwhelming visitors.

## What we built

A clean, corporate design with a bold red-and-navy palette that matches Specom's brand. The homepage starts with the brands they're authorized to represent, followed by a rotating hero that introduces their core solutions. From there, services, industries served, reasons to choose Specom, a project gallery and FAQs guide visitors toward an enquiry.

The site includes a language selector and a live chat option. It's hand-coded for speed and structured for search.
