## The goal

Singing Chef Adventures offers food, music and heritage tours around Georgetown and beyond. Most guests plan their trip from overseas, so the website has to do the work of a first conversation: show who Eon is, what each tour involves, what it costs, and make booking feel easy.

## What we built

We chose a warm, editorial look, with cream tones, a classic serif and rich food photography, to match the hospitality of the tours themselves. The homepage leads with Eon and a rotating line ("Experience the real taste / culture / music / history of Guyana") and then presents the signature experiences as a clear, browsable set of tours with prices.

Guest reviews sit next to the tours, and a strong "Book your adventure" call to action repeats through the page. On mobile, call and WhatsApp buttons stay fixed at the bottom of the screen.

The site is hand-coded, fast and search-friendly, so travellers looking for food and culture tours in Guyana can find it.
